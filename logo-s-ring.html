Ring around it.

You knew what you wanted — five executions of the same idea, each with a different personality. The "S" stays the hero, the ring frames it. Pick the geometry that feels most "you."

Variation A

Classic Seal

Solid indigo orb, clean white S inside, single ring framing it like a stamp.

Timeless · Authoritative
Designer's note The most timeless of the variations — reads as "official seal of an academy." The ring acts as a frame, the gap between ring and sphere creates breathing room (the "halo"). This is the safest, most graduate-coded execution. Will look gorgeous on certificates.

Variation B

Outlined Ring

Ring as outline, no fill — S floats inside in full indigo gradient. Lighter, more modern.

Modern · Minimal
Designer's note Same idea as Variation A but inverted weight: instead of solid sphere with white S, we get an open ring containing a gradient S. Feels lighter, more contemporary, more "tech startup." Perfect when you want the S to feel like the protagonist, not a passenger.

Variation C

Planet Ring

Like Saturn — sphere with a tilted orbital ring around it. Cosmic, distinctive.

Cosmic · Storytelling
Designer's note This is the most narratively rich. The ring is no longer just a frame — it's an orbit. The S sits at the heart of a planet, encircled by its student satellites/journey. Direct nod to "Stratos" (sky/space). The ellipse tilt adds dimension and movement that flat circles can't.

Variation D

Two rings at different angles — atomic energy. The S sits at the nucleus.

Variation E

Halo

Sphere with a thin floating ring above it — like a graduate's cap halo. Subtle but iconic.

Iconic · Memorable
Designer's note Different from the rest — the ring isn't around the whole sphere, it's floating above it like a halo. Reads as "graduate," "achievement," "blessed." Subtle but the silhouette is unforgettable. Think Notion's icon energy: just a ball and a cap. Quiet brilliance.

Designer's pick

My honest call:

1
Variation A — Classic Seal

If you only pick one, pick this. Survives every size. Looks correct everywhere — navbar, favicon, certificate, app icon. The ring as a frame is the most timeless treatment. Lowest risk, highest "feels right."

2
Variation C — Planet Ring

If you want personality. The Saturn-style tilted ring tells the Stratos story without explaining anything. Highest distinctiveness — no one will confuse this with another LMS.

3
Variation E — Halo

Wildcard pick. Reads as "graduate halo" — perfectly on-message for an academy. The single floating ring above the sphere is unforgettable once you see it. Most emotionally resonant.

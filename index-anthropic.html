Questions

Everything before you sign up.

Do I need marketing experience?
Nope. Phase 1 starts from absolute zero — we cover the basics of digital marketing, image creatives, and brand identity. By the time you reach Phase 4 (Meta Ads), you'll have built up enough foundation to launch real campaigns.
How long does the program really take?
It's a 16-lesson, self-paced program. Active students finish in ~4 months at one lesson per week. You can also race through — some grads complete it in 6 to 8 weeks.
Is this free or paid?
Free to start, free to finish. The core 16-lesson program is permanently free. We may add premium features later (mentorship, paid portfolio reviews) but the curriculum stays open.
Can I do this with a full-time job?
Yes — each lesson takes 30 to 60 minutes plus weekly assignments. Most students study 3 to 5 hours per week. Lessons are recorded so you can watch anytime.
Is the certificate recognized?
It's a verifiable Sphere Academy by Stratos certificate with a public URL graduates can share. It's not a government-accredited diploma, but it's recognized by Stratos partner companies and our hiring network.
How is this different from free YouTube?
YouTube gives you scattered tips with no structure, no assignments, no community, no feedback. Sphere gives you a 16-week roadmap, weekly hands-on work, a cohort of peers, and a graduating outcome. Both are valuable — Sphere takes you further.

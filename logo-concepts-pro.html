Concept 07

The Hidden "S" Sphere

A sphere with an S secretly carved through the negative space — FedEx-arrow level cleverness.

Sophisticated
Designer's note Most logos shout. The smart ones whisper. Once a student spots the S inside the orb — that "oh!" moment — they never unsee it. That's the moment your brand becomes memorable. The mark works as a pure sphere first, S second. Two readings, one logo.

Concept 08

The Aperture

A camera iris in mid-rotation. Marketing is perspective — this mark is the lens.

Photographic
Designer's note Marketing students learn to see. The aperture mark literalizes that — six blades, each a perspective, converging on the truth at center. Bonus: it photographs beautifully on apparel, embossed on certificates, animated as an opening shutter for video stings.

Concept 09

Stratosphere

Atmospheric bands stacked into a sphere — your parent brand made literal.

Brand-storied
Designer's note "Stratos" comes from stratosphere — the layered sky. This mark visualizes exactly that: atmospheric strata stacked from light (surface) to deep (space). Reading top-to-bottom feels like ascending through phases of mastery. The sphere shape is implied by the rounded clip — geometry doing the storytelling.

Concept 10

Four Phases Bloom

Four petals — one per phase. The flower opens as students progress.

Living mark
Designer's note Your program has exactly 4 phases. This mark is those 4 phases — petals around a core, each in its own indigo shade. The radical idea: the logo could literally unfold on a student's profile as they complete each phase. Phase 1 done = first petal blooms. By graduation, the full flower. Logo as progress indicator.

Concept 12

Designer's Final Picks

If I had to bet my reputation on three.

1
Concept 07 — The Hidden "S" Sphere

Pure brand mnemonic. The "oh, it's an S!" moment is what gets your logo screenshotted and shared. This is the kind of mark that wins design awards and survives 20 years. Highest ceiling.

2
Concept 10 — Four Phases Bloom

Most strategic. Your logo literally becomes the student's progress. Phase 1 done = first petal. By graduation, full bloom. No other school's logo can do this. Marketing genius applied to your own brand.

3
Concept 09 — Stratosphere

Most on-brand. Your parent brand is literally named after this concept. Each band = a phase. Each gradient stop = a level of mastery. Tells the whole story without a single word.
